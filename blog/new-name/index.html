A new name. The same promise.

We have a new name.

The Ghana Hypertension Screening Initiative (GHSI) is now the Community Hypertension Initiative in Ghana, a program of Blessed Health Foundation LBG.

The Foundation was incorporated in Ghana this September and is now the legal home of our work.

A stopped heart cannot build a nation.

Mavis Amegah-DorrFounder and Executive Director

Bringing accessible blood pressure screening and health education to Ghana's transport hub communities. Community-driven. Evidence-based. Life-saving.
