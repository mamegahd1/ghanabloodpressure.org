Thank you for 2026

What we built together this year, and where we go in 2027.

As the year closes, here is what we built together at the Community Hypertension Initiative in Ghana (formerly the Ghana Hypertension Screening Initiative, GHSI), a program of Blessed Health Foundation LBG, with our team, our advisors and our partners.

What we built in 2026

  • Team

    Our team of clinicians, researchers and community leads grew this year. Our advisors gave their time to review our work and to work alongside us.

  • Partners

    Akoma+ Health and Wellness Foundation is our screening partner. Our partnership with The Public Health Alliance International Ghana (PHAIG) was formalized in April, and Central University became our academic partner this year.

  • A home for the work

    Blessed Health Foundation LBG was incorporated in Ghana on 17 September. In the United States, Blessed Health Foundation USA, Inc. is now recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity.

  • Curriculum

    Our training curriculum entered its next phase, co-led by Ms. Anaba and Dr. Amu.

  • Follow-up

    We built Stay, our system for following each person found at risk for 12 months, on DHIS2.

  • Network

    We became an Associate Member of the World Hypertension League.

  • Giving

    Our first year-end campaign, Fund the Staying, opened on 14 November.

Looking ahead

Our goals for 2027

  1. 1

    Curriculum

    Publish the training curriculum as a vetted guide.

  2. 2

    Stay

    Prepare Stay for its first users: hosting in Ghana and registration with Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

  3. 3

    Funding

    Secure funding to link 1,000+ people already screened to care, and keep them there for a year.
