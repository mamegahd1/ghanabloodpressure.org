The GHSI app, for the team

This page is for GHSI volunteers, nurses, callers and coordinators. It gives you the app link and shows how to put the app on your phone.

Open the app

Tap the button, or scan the code with your phone camera. Both take you to the same place.

Open the GHSI app

ghanabloodpressure.org/app

Put it on your phone (Android)

Do this once. After that, the app opens from your home screen like any other app, even with weak network.

  1. 1
    Open the link

    Tap the button above, or type ghanabloodpressure.org/app in Chrome.

  2. 2
    Sign in

    Use the username and password on your login card. Ask your Site Lead if you do not have a card yet.

  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen

    Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome, then tap "Add to Home screen", then tap "Add".

  4. 4
    Open from the roundel

    From now on, tap the round GHSI icon on your home screen to open the app. Do not open it from Chrome again.
